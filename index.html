English

Tiny Lobby is an indie studio making short story games you play free in the browser, on a phone or a desktop.

La Abuela: you're the grandma, and a scammer is calling: first "your grandson", then a lawyer, then an inspector. Your job is not to hang up. Listen, pick your answers and catch him in his own lie. Spanish comedy with voiced calls and several endings; also in English and Ukrainian. Play on itch.io.

Night Desk: a free noir interactive fiction. You are the night clerk of a small hotel; a woman in grey leaves an envelope and two hundred dollars for a favour. Six nights, 48 endings, 15–25 minutes a night, 16+. Voiced in English and Spanish; text in English, Spanish and Ukrainian. Play on itch.io.
